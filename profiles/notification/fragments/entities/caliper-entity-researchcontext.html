A Caliper Notice entity represents a notable remark or alert that a user, or system, sends to another user to draw their attention to an important matter.

A Notice consists of the data of a notable remark to send; it can be dispatched to more than one user. When dispatched to a user, it must be contained within a Notification" envelope.

A Caliper ResearchContext entity identifies the educational research context within which a notification occurs, allowing for subsequent collation of all notification event data that pertains to the context.
